Front Door Intelligence

A Ring doorbell press becomes one sentence of plain English on your TV. Amazon Developer Hackathon 2026 — Ring track. Both demos below run fixture data through the production code; nothing at a real front door was recorded.

Fire TV overlay — the moment What a television shows when the doorbell rings: the sentence, the snapshot, a 15-second auto-dismiss, and a dismiss button. New fixture events arrive every few seconds. Web dashboard — the day What the homeowner reviews at the end of the day: every event, its snapshot, and a summary of the day, with pending events shown honestly.
